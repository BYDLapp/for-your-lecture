Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

For Your Lecture is a note-taking app for iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we don't collect anything.

What we collect

Nothing. For Your Lecture has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The app never sends any data to us, and we have no servers that could receive it.

Your notes

Your notes, their titles and page settings are stored on your iPad. If you're signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive turned on, the app also keeps them in your own private iCloud storage, so they're there when you reinstall the app or move to a new iPad. That storage belongs to your Apple Account: we can't see or access it.

iCloud is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. If iCloud Drive is off, your notes stay only on your iPad.

Deleting your data

You can delete a note at any time by long-pressing it and choosing Delete; it's removed from your iPad and your iCloud. To remove everything, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, choose For Your Lecture and delete its data, then delete the app.

Children

For Your Lecture doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if we add a feature that needs data), we'll update this page and the date above before that version of the app is released.

Contact

For Your Lecture is made by Galip Alp Demirel (Ankara, Türkiye). Questions? Email support@bydl.app.
