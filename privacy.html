Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

For Your Lecture is a note-taking app for iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we don't collect anything.

What we collect

Nothing. For Your Lecture has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The app doesn't connect to the internet and doesn't send any data to us or to anyone else.

Your notes

Your notes, their titles and page settings are stored only on your iPad, inside the app. We can't see them, and they never leave your device through the app.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPad to a computer, Apple may include the app's data in those backups. That's handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

Deleting your data

You can delete a note at any time by long-pressing it and choosing Delete. Deleting the app removes all of its notes from your iPad.

Children

For Your Lecture doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if we add a feature that needs data), we'll update this page and the date above before that version of the app is released.
