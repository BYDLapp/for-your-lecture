Quick answers

How do I rename or delete a note?
Long-press the note in your library and choose Rename or Delete. You can also tap the title at the top of an open note to rename it.

How do I change the paper?
Open a note and tap the page icon in the top-right corner: Blank, Lined, Grid or Dotted.

My finger draws instead of scrolling.
Scroll with two fingers, or turn on Settings → Apple Pencil → Only Draw with Apple Pencil so one finger scrolls.

Where are my notes saved?
Notes save automatically while you write. They're kept on your iPad and, if iCloud Drive is on, in your own iCloud.

Will my notes come with me to a new iPad?
Yes, as long as you're signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive turned on. Install For Your Lecture on the new iPad and your notes appear after a moment. The same goes for deleting and reinstalling the app.

My notes don't show up on my other device.
Check that both devices use the same Apple Account and that iCloud Drive is on. If the library says "iCloud storage is full", your notes are safe on this device but can't upload until you free up space in Settings → [your name] → iCloud, or upgrade your iCloud storage. They sync on their own once there's room.

I use two iPads. Do notes sync?
Yes, through iCloud. If you edit the same note on both at the same moment, the last save wins.
